# Competitor dossier — verified primary-source findings

Companion to `docs/COMPETITOR-LANDSCAPE.md` (the 38-country market map).
This file holds **deep, citable findings from the providers' own published
material** — coverage sheets, methodology pages, whitepapers, association
data programmes.

**Method and its limits, stated plainly.** Everything here comes from public
pages: vendor coverage PDFs, marketing pages, whitepapers, and association
publications. Nothing was taken from behind a login or paywall, and no
provider's entity/fund/deal records were extracted — what is recorded is what
each vendor *says publicly about itself*, plus the public primary sources they
name. That is the useful part anyway: it tells us which registers, filings and
FOIA routes to take directly.

Research was cut short when the session's web-search quota was exhausted
(200/200). Direct URL fetching still worked, so the Preqin and Invest Europe

## Preqin (BlackRock) — the benchmark, and its soft spots

Source: Preqin's own **Global Data Coverage sheet, June 2025**
([PDF](https://assets.ctfassets.net/zf87m07ner47/1z6rN7WM0kAVo1xCfJt0Ya/4ca8d328ea78ecb7a18702a2a178bae2/Preqin_Data_Coverage_-_June_2025.PDF)).

### Stated coverage

| Metric | Count |
|---|---|
| Firms | 64,621 |
| Funds | 220,111 |
| Funds open to investment | 56,250 |
| Investors monitored | 36,249 |
| Funds with performance data | 65,351 |
| Deals & exits | 795,060 |
| Service-provider **relationships** | 445,440+ |
| Contacts | 558,019 |
| Consultants | 637 |

### Where their data comes from (stated verbatim)

- **FOIA requests to US public pension plans** — named: CalPERS, Washington
  State Investment Board, Florida SBA (private capital); Teacher Retirement
  System of Texas, Ohio PERS (hedge funds).
- **Voluntary GP submissions** — ~24,000 submissions/year from 6,900+ GPs.
- **Hedge fund manager factsheets** — ~2,000 managers supplying monthly
  factsheets on ~3,944 funds.
- **News monitoring** — 230,000 news sources, 220 jurisdictions, 120 languages.
- **Listed-company financial reports, public filings, annual reports.**

### Three findings worth acting on

1. **Their EMEA research capacity is thin.** Stated research headcount is
   **526 total: 389 APAC, 72 EMEA, 65 Americas**. Seventy-two researchers
   cover all of Europe, the Middle East and Africa. Depth in CEE/SEE is not a
   thing they are structurally staffed to do.
2. **Their own numbers disagree.** The `/data/our-data` marketing page claims
   "48,000 firms, 135,000+ funds, 20,000+ investors" while the June 2025
   coverage sheet says 64,621 / 220,111 / 36,249 — same vendor, same period,
   materially different figures.
3. **They concede the self-reporting gap.** Preqin's own whitepaper
   ([*Don't Believe the Hype*, Feb 2019](https://docs.preqin.com/reports/Preqin-Don't-Believe-the-Hype-Whitepaper-February-2019.pdf))
   states that funds submitting data represent "more than 25% of the known
   fund universe" — i.e. ~75% does not self-report — and that "there is no
   such thing as the 'perfect' dataset."

### Stated scope limit

"We do not track trade deals or other M&As" — corporate M&A outside sponsor
activity is out of scope for them.

### What this means for Continuum

Preqin's spine is **US public-pension FOIA + voluntary GP submissions**.
Neither route produces European entity ground truth. Our spine — national
commercial registers, regulator licence registers, gazettes, insolvency
courts — is the one thing their model structurally under-serves, and it is
exactly where the CEE/SEE launch depth sits.

## Invest Europe — the free European alternative to a paid database

The **European Data Cooperative (EDC)**
([about](https://www.investeurope.eu/research/about-research/data-collection/))
aggregates, from national association contributions:

- 4,000 firms · 10,900 funds · 86,700 portfolio companies · 330,900 transactions

Contributing national associations include **CVCA (Czechia), PSIK (Poland),
SEEPEA (Romania/Bulgaria)** among others — i.e. the CEE associations feed a
single pan-European dataset. The
[2025 Central and Eastern Europe Private Equity Statistics report](https://www.investeurope.eu/publications-policy/publications/2026/2025-central-and-eastern-europe-private-equity-statistics/)
is **free to download**.

This is a genuinely free, association-grade benchmark for CEE market sizing —
worth citing in research output and worth reconciling our entity counts
against. It is aggregate statistics, not an entity-level database, so it
complements rather than competes with the register spine.
